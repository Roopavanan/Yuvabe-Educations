# **Yuvabe Education – Foundation Programme**

**Build Confidence. Gain Clarity. Find Your Direction.**  
The Yuvabe Foundation Programme is a free 6-week learning journey designed to help young adults build essential life, communication, digital, and career skills. Sessions are held every Saturday with two interactive learning sessions each day.

## **Programme Highlights**

* Duration: 6 Weeks  
* Schedule: Every Saturday (Two Sessions per Day)  
* Tuition: Free  
* Lunch Provided  
* Limited to 20 Participants  
* Venue: SaraCon, Auroville

## **What You'll Learn**

**Confident Communication:** Reading, writing, listening, speaking and presentation skills.

**Digital & Practical Skills:** Digital design basics and practical digital tools.

**Critical Thinking & Problem Solving:** Analytical thinking and practical problem solving.

**Career Readiness:** Resume preparation, career planning and interview readiness.

## **6-Week Curriculum**

* Week 1: Orientation, Self-Awareness, Goal Setting & Personal Development  
* Week 2: Communication Essentials – Reading, Writing, Listening & Speaking  
* Week 3: Digital Skills – Digital Design Basics & Practical Tools  
* Week 4: AI for Learning & Productivity – AI Tools & ChatGPT  
* Week 5: Time Management, Planning & Communication Activities  
* Week 6: Resume Preparation, Career Development, Individual Presentation & Graduation

## **Who Can Join?**

* Anyone aged 18–35  
* Students  
* Fresh Graduates  
* Job Seekers  
* Young Professionals

## **Learning Outcomes**

* Communicate confidently.  
* Use digital and AI tools effectively.  
* Manage time efficiently.  
* Create a professional resume.  
* Present ideas with confidence.  
* Build a strong foundation for future studies and careers.
